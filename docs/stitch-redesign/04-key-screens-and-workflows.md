# Key screens and workflows

## Priority screens for concept exploration

1. **Home dashboard** — Household or individual overview with net worth, portfolio, tracked spending, savings/investing, goals, trends, and quick actions.
2. **Budget** — Select a partner and period, review income and allocations, manage grouped categories, and understand assigned versus remaining money.
3. **Expenses** — Log a transaction quickly, monitor category/envelope progress, review the period's transactions, and reconcile planned versus actual money.
4. **Investments** — Review portfolio value and performance, understand account types and freshness of market data, then inspect or update holdings.
5. **Purchase Advisor** — Enter a prospective purchase and compare affordability paths such as saving, installments, or temporary budget adjustments before applying anything.

## Supporting areas

- Banks and balance updates
- Net-worth composition and history
- Goals and contributions
- Installments and payment status
- Shared household expenses and bills
- Currency conversion
- Settings, navigation customization, backup/sync, appearance, and pay-period configuration

## Important cross-app behavior

- Switch between each partner and the combined household where appropriate
- Move from summaries into the relevant detailed view without losing context
- Add transactions and contributions from multiple entry points
- Navigate and enter data comfortably on a phone
- Show offline, syncing, stale market data, conflict, and destructive-action states clearly
- Keep common tasks fast while allowing complex financial detail to remain available
