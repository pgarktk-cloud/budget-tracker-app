# Product context

## What the product is

Where'dItGo is a private, mobile-first household finance PWA. It helps a couple plan salary, allocate money, record spending, prepare for purchases, and understand their combined or individual financial position.

The experience should make complex household finances feel understandable and actionable—not like accounting software.

## Audience and usage

- Two partners managing personal and jointly owned finances
- Frequent mobile use for transactions, budget checks, and quick decisions
- Deeper periodic review of budgets, investments, goals, bills, and net worth
- Users who benefit from visual, ADHD-friendly presentation with low cognitive load

## Core capabilities

- Salary and pay-period budgeting with grouped categories
- Expense logging, transfers, extra funds, and period reconciliation
- Bank accounts and multi-type investment tracking
- Net-worth history, assets, and liabilities
- Savings goals and contributions
- Shared household expenses and bill tracking
- Installment management and purchase-affordability planning
- Individual partner and combined household views

## Product constraints

- Preserve existing financial functionality and calculation meaning
- Mobile-first, touch-friendly, responsive, and installable as a PWA
- Must work offline and communicate automatic sync status clearly
- Support both strong light and dark experiences
- Handle empty, partial, loading, stale-data, offline, and error states
- The current frontend has no build step and is implemented as a single React/Babel page; the concept should remain realistically implementable in a web UI
