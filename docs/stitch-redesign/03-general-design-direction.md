# General design direction

## Chosen direction

Create a hybrid of **Calm Visual Finance** and **Warm & Playful Finance**.

The result should feel polished, reassuring, approachable, and motivating. It should have the clarity of a premium personal-finance product without feeling corporate, sterile, childish, or game-like.

## Experience principles

- ADHD-friendly: one obvious priority at a time and limited competing focal points
- Glanceable: communicate financial state through meaningful charts, progress graphics, comparisons, and status cues
- Calm: preserve breathing room even on information-rich screens
- Actionable: make it clear what is healthy, what needs attention, and what the user can do next
- Progressive: reveal secondary detail when needed instead of presenting everything at once
- Consistent: use a coherent visual and interaction language across daily and analytical workflows
- Human: add warmth through color, language, and visual rhythm—not illustrations

## Color and themes

Explore a strong, purpose-designed light mode and dark mode. The existing navy/slate, jade green, supporting blue, and muted red palette is a useful starting point and may be refined rather than discarded.

## Creative freedom

Stitch should make the detailed visual decisions. It may propose new hierarchy, navigation, layouts, component patterns, and interaction models where they improve usability. Do not treat current neumorphism, glass effects, card shapes, or layout conventions as requirements.

Use data visualization and progress graphics, not decorative character illustration. Avoid turning every number into a chart or creating an overwhelming dashboard.
