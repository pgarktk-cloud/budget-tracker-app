# Google Stitch master prompt

Redesign **Where'dItGo**, a private, mobile-first household finance PWA for a couple. It combines salary/pay-period budgeting, expense tracking and reconciliation, banks, investments, net worth, savings goals, shared expenses, bills, installments, and purchase-affordability planning. Relevant screens and product context are included in the accompanying documents and screenshots.

Develop a fresh visual concept rather than merely reskinning the current interface. Reinterpret the product's hierarchy, navigation, layouts, and interaction patterns while preserving its important functionality and the distinction between each partner and the combined household.

The broad creative direction is a hybrid of **Calm Visual Finance** and **Warm & Playful Finance**: polished, reassuring, approachable, motivating, and easy to understand without feeling corporate, sterile, childish, or game-like. Design for ADHD-friendly use with clear priorities, limited competing focal points, predictable actions, progressive disclosure, and strong at-a-glance comprehension.

Use meaningful data visualization and progress graphics to make financial status easier and more enjoyable to understand. Prefer visual explanation over walls of raw data, but do not turn every number into a chart or create dashboard clutter. Do not use decorative character illustrations.

Explore strong, intentional light and dark modes. The current navy/slate, jade green, supporting blue, and muted red colors may be refined as a starting palette, but do not be constrained by the current component styling, neumorphism, glass effects, card treatments, or layout.

Please:

- Explore and propose a distinctive, fresh visual concept
- Make your own detailed design decisions where appropriate
- Improve hierarchy, navigation, layout, usability, and visual polish
- Create a coherent design language across daily and analytical workflows
- Feel free to propose better layouts and interaction patterns
- Preserve essential product functionality and financial meaning
- Prioritize mobile and touch use while showing how the concept responds to larger screens
- Account for empty, loading, offline, syncing, stale-data, error, and destructive-action states
- Produce a strong concept we can evaluate and refine afterward, not a final implementation specification

Start with a coherent concept across these representative screens: Home, Budget, Expenses, Investments, and Purchase Advisor. Show enough shared navigation and secondary-action behavior to demonstrate how the wider product would fit the same system. Use the supplied current screenshots as product-reference material, not as layouts that must be copied.
